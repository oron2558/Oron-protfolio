02  How I work

One person, the whole arc.

I don't hand off between the parts. Research, product definition, UI and the launch are all mine, so nothing gets lost in between.

  1. 01

    Discovery

    Frame the real problem before touching a screen: interviews, research and the why behind the build.

  2. 02

    Insights and journeys

    Map where people struggle today, and turn the findings into decisions a team can act on.

  3. 03

    Characterization

    Translate strategy into requirements, user flows and buildable UI: what to build and why, not only how it looks.

  4. 04

    Build with AI

    I prototype and ship working software fast with AI tools such as Claude Code and Lovable, not just mockups.

  5. 05

    Ship and iterate

    Launch with paid campaigns, then improve from real user feedback. Years in advertising taught me adoption is part of design.
