The problem — and the whole purpose

For a couple, everything is scattered across suppliers

Planning a wedding, a couple ends up juggling a different supplier for each piece — one company for RSVP calls, another for seating, another for gifting — on top of their own WhatsApp groups and spreadsheets. Nothing talks to anything else, so they lose track of who's coming, rebuild seating by hand, and drown in follow-ups.

The entire purpose of Meashrim in Click: bring every piece into one simple, clear system — so the couple stays in control instead of getting tangled up, and actually stays on top of their own event.

Scattered — a supplier for everything
  • One supplier for RSVP calls, another for seating, another for gifts
  • Guest replies lost across WhatsApp groups and spreadsheets
  • Seating rebuilt by hand every time someone answers
  • No single, live view of where the event actually stands
One house — Meashrim in Click
  • RSVP, seating and gifting in one system
  • One shareable link — guests confirm in a single tap
  • Live dashboard: confirmed, undecided, not-coming, no-reply
  • Seating and billable-guest counts update automatically

Market research

What the market survey revealed

I surveyed seven RSVP providers across 2024. Rather than reprint the table, here's what it actually told me — and where the opening was.

~₪1.8
Average price per guest record for RSVP collection (range ₪0.9–2.32).
₪1,500–2,300
Seating sold separately, as a human service — per event.
Per-message
Everyone bills per record / per blast, event by event.
What the market includes

RSVP collection only — rounds of WhatsApp messages and phone calls, SMS reminders and a thank-you. Some add credit-card gifting. Seating is a separate, pricey human service, and it's all billed by the guest record.

What we give — one house
  • Arrival approvals (אישורי הגעה) — RSVP collection & live tracking
  • Seating (סידורי הושבה) — built in, not a separate service
  • Credit-card gifting (מתנות באשראי)
  • All self-serve, one subscription, one place
Go-to-market pricing

I launched with a deliberately low entry (penetration) price — set below the per-record market — to pull customers away from the incumbents and build a base fast, then grow value from there.

What competitors don't have — that we do
  • Built-in seating (theirs is a separate ₪1,500–2,300 service)
  • One product bundling RSVP + seating + gifting
  • A flat subscription instead of per-message billing
  • Self-serve control — live dashboard, auto-seating, no middleman

How I approached it

A strategist's cross-view, not a pretty screen

I read the market, characterized the product, defined what to build for the user — then connected the three by solving the real problem. That's product & project management, end to end.

Coming from years of advertising and project management, I didn't start from a UI — I started from the business and the behavior. Who is the host, what decision are they stuck on, where does the money leak, and what is the one thing the product has to nail. The app is the output of that thinking, not the starting point.

Characterization → product

What I specced and shipped

The spec turned into a focused product with a clear job: give the host total control of arrivals, seating and money — in one place.

Real-time RSVP dashboard
Confirmed, undecided, not-coming and no-reply — with a live “how far along are we” progress ring.
Automatic seating
Seating and billable-guest counts recalculate as replies come in — no more manual spreadsheet rebuilds.
Suppliers & payments
Track vendors and payment status alongside the guest list, so the budget and the event stay in sync.
Multi-event admin
A back-office that manages events, users, suppliers and subscriptions — the system behind the product.
Smart reminders
Timed nudges — upload the guest list 30 days out, organize seating a week before — so nothing slips.
One-tap approval link
A shareable link lets guests confirm without installing anything — the lowest-friction path to a reply.
Deep-dive: the seating system, fully characterized
One feature, specced end-to-end before a single screen was designed — a sample of the characterization work behind the product.
  • Create tables by type (round / square / knights) with seats per table
  • Live table list with fill status — full, partial, empty — edit & duplicate
  • Guest panel grouped by RSVP status: coming, undecided, not-coming, no-reply
  • Search by name or phone; assign by tap or drag
  • One-tap auto-seating by family into shared tables by availability
  • Two-way sync between a seat and each guest's “table number” field
  • Day-of “arrived” check-in per guest
  • PDF / Excel export, plus a venue-sketch upload so we plan seating without bugging the client
