HI.

01  My story

From campaigns to products.

Leading advertising projects is the same discipline as product work in tech. I simply swapped campaigns for products.

Before

Marketing, advertising and business strategy

Several years leading projects, managing client relationships, coordinating cross-functional teams and turning business goals into measurable results, including multimillion-shekel campaigns.

The switch

Into UX/UI

A natural next step, driven by understanding people and solving complex problems. User research, product requirements, user flows and close work with designers and developers.

Building

Founder mode

Building and improving my own digital products with AI, automation and no-code tools. It taught me to balance user needs, business strategy, technical feasibility and execution.

Now

Two products live in the market

Meashrim in Click is in the App Store with 486 registered users, a 5.0 rating and paying subscribers. MyPlanner is a wedding-planning system in real use. I'm looking for a product role in a UX-driven team.
